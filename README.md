## How These Write-ups Are Structured

Each write-up follows a consistent investigation-report format:

1. **Scenario** — the incident/alert framing
2. **Objective** — what needed to be determined
3. **Tools Used**
4. **Investigation & Analysis** — reasoning and pivots, not just steps
5. **Findings** — IOCs, techniques, MITRE ATT&CK mapping
6. **Impact Assessment**
7. **Recommendations** — detection/remediation guidance

No flags, credentials, or literal payloads are posted verbatim — findings are described analytically, consistent with platform terms of service.
